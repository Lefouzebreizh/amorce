Children's picture-book illustration in RICH, SATURATED WATERCOLOUR with bold
confident black ink linework. Strong tonal contrast, vivid autumn colours,
crisp well-defined outlines. Clean cream background.

Do NOT overlay any paper grain, aging, sepia wash, canvas texture or vintage
filter on top of the artwork — it flattens the colours and softens every edge.
The artwork must look freshly painted and sharp.

ROUSSY: a small slender red fox cub, deep copper-orange fur, white chest and
white tail tip, warm amber eyes, dark brown paws, expressive eyebrows.
Quadruped fox anatomy, never humanised.

ZEPHY: a small winged zebra foal. Realistic equine anatomy — four hooves, no
human torso, no hands. Crisp black and white stripes. Mane and tail tuft in
DEEP VIOLET and RICH GOLD. LARGE spread feathered wings, deep violet at the top
fading to warm gold at the tips — vivid and saturated, never pale lavender.
Comic, rubbery, highly expressive face; tongue out, eyes crossed.

LAYOUT: square image, a 2x2 grid of four equal panels, each panel framed by a
thin simple double line. Narrow cream margin around the grid. Below the grid, an
empty unfurled parchment scroll.

Panel 1: mossy autumn forest, ROUSSY sitting alone on a mossy stump, ears down,
eyes sad, small mushrooms and fallen leaves around him.
Panel 2: bright blue sky with soft white clouds, ZEPHY flying with an opened
banana skin draped over his head like a wig, tongue out, gurning, wings wide.
Panel 3: sunny green meadow, ROUSSY throwing his head back laughing, ZEPHY
sitting beside him grinning, a ladybird on the grass.
Panel 4: same meadow, both laughing together, ROUSSY sitting up on his hind legs
with paws in the air, ZEPHY laughing with wings raised, wildflowers and a spider
on the grass.

  LES DEUX CONSIGNES QUI COMPTENT PLUS QUE TOUT LE RESTE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1.  AUCUN TEXTE NULLE PART. No title, no speech bubbles, no lettering, no
    words, no letters, no numbers, no panel numbers, no signature, no
    watermark. The parchment scroll must be COMPLETELY BLANK.

2.  AUCUNE BORDURE DÉCORATIVE. No leaves, no acorns, no feathers, no
    ornamental frame around the page. Only the four panels, their thin double
    frames, and the blank scroll, on plain cream. The book's own border will be
    added afterwards.

Leave the UPPER THIRD of each panel visually calm — open sky, plain foliage or
empty ground — with the characters in the lower two thirds. That space receives
the speech bubbles.

Render as a SQUARE image at the highest resolution available — 2600 x 2600
pixels or more. Generate large; do not upscale a small image. If the tool offers
a quality, detail or steps setting, put it at maximum.

`greffe.py` pose le contenu dans le cadre du volume — bordure exacte au pixel.
`lettrage.py` trace titre, bulles, médaillons et parchemin. Ajouter --reperes
pour vérifier d'abord qu'aucune bulle ne couvre le personnage qui parle.
